# Files that often come with PowerPoint decks

- Office Viewer Pack: Adds the other three Office viewers in one install.
- DOCX Viewer (.docx files): Previews Word and ODT files with dark mode, an outline and search.
- XLSX and CSV Viewer (.xlsx, .csv or .parquet files): Opens CSV, TSV, Excel and Parquet files as a sortable, filterable table.

Each one is a separate free extension. Nothing installs on its own.
