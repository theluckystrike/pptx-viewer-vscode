# Search slides and notes

Press `Ctrl+F` (`Cmd+F` on macOS) while a deck is open and type a word.

- The Outline tab lists each slide whose title, text, table cells or speaker notes match.
- Matches are marked in the outline and in the notes.
- `Enter` jumps to the next matching slide, `Shift+Enter` to the previous one, and `Esc` clears the search.
